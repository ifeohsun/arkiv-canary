🐤 CROPS Canary

Self-expiring, signed warrant canaries stored on Arkiv (Tiramisu testnet). A canary is alive only while its entity exists on Arkiv. If the publisher stops renewing it, Arkiv expires it and the canary dies on its own. Nobody, the publisher included, can quietly edit or remove it.
